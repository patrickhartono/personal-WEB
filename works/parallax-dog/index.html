Parallax Dog

Video mapping work · SUMONAR Festival, Yogyakarta

A video mapping work exploring the parallax technique to build a three-dimensional optical illusion out of a flat surface. A moving dog model functions as a Z-axis reference point, giving the audience a depth cue to read into what is, physically, a building facade. Designed to project directly onto architecture — the building's own surface becomes the canvas the illusion is built from — with audio-reactive processes driving the visual changes in real time from the accompanying music.

Presented in the video mapping programme at SUMONAR, the Yogyakarta electronic music and digital arts festival — the same festival that later hosted SpecGliFed (2021).
