Works That Read You

Mycortex

Interactive installation · with Wang Zhiqi · 2025

An installation exploring non-human symbolic communication through bioelectric signals — reading electrical activity from living organic material and treating it as a communicative channel rather than raw data to be visualised and discarded.

The premise sits alongside Gorgon Loop and Jakarta Witness in asking what a machine does with a signal it is given — here the signal originates outside the human register entirely.
