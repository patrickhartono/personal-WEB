Handmate

Browser-based controller · with Melody Lim, Nefeli Kotsani · 2022

A browser-based controller for Web Audio and MIDI, using AI hand-tracking in place of dedicated hardware — no sensor to buy, no driver to install, just a camera and a webpage standing in for a MIDI controller.

The accessibility argument is literal: the barrier Handmate removes is the cost and availability of physical controllers, which is precisely the constraint that recurs across this practice's interface work. It remains the most-cited item in the record.
