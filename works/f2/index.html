F2

Electroacoustic composition, generative volumetric visuals, text as compositional agent · 2025

F2 works with historical memory and the difficulty of public remembrance in Indonesia. Sound, generative image and text are built as a single compositional system, with text functioning as a structuring material — shaping duration, density and silence — rather than as a caption to the image.

The visual material is held inside a slowly rotating volumetric frame, a container that never quite resolves into a whole. The work does not reconstruct what happened. It makes a space in which what was taken can be felt.
