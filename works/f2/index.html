Fixed-Media Audiovisual

F2

Audiovisual composition · electroacoustic sound, generative visuals, text · 2025

F2 engages with historical memory and the difficulty of public remembrance in Indonesia, taking the May 1998 riots as its material. Sound, generative image and text are built as a single compositional system, with text treated as a structuring element within the composition rather than as illustration laid over it.

A generative point-cloud environment — the visual register of the piece — runs alongside the historical text, rendering the event as a volatile, reconstructive image rather than a fixed archival photograph.
