Live Works with Machines

Cici Celine

Immersive web performance · with Yola Yulfianti · 2026

No venue hardware, no dedicated screens: a performer's movement is tracked live and translated into audiovisual output that plays directly on each audience member's own phone, turning the room into a distributed sound and image field rather than a stage the audience faces.

The piece asks what immersive performance becomes when it is built for devices people already carry, rather than for equipment much of the region cannot afford — continuing a collaboration with choreographer Yola Yulfianti that runs back to Angkot Is the Melting Pot in 2017.
