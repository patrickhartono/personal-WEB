Works That Read You

Gorgon Loop

Interactive installation · with Shuang Li · 2026

Five rotatable "intelligent mirrors" turn the exhibition's own gaze back on its visitors. Machine vision reads whoever stands in front of a panel; the reading is converted into structured parameters and handed to a generative language agent, which produces evaluative commentary in real time — the mirror does not just show you, it judges you, in the cold, confident register of an algorithm that has never been asked to explain itself.

Developed with Shuang Li, whose research question and technical infrastructure this project extended into a peer-reviewed art paper — the installation makes an argument usually confined to a methods section: that classification is never neutral, and that the mirror is the wrong metaphor for what these systems actually do.
