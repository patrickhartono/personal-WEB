Jakarta Witness

Browser-based installation · runs entirely client-side · 2026

Real Jakarta traffic footage runs through an object-detection network, its bounding boxes counting motorbikes, cars and pedestrians in real time. A small language model reads those counts and proposes solutions in a cold, clinical voice — logical on its own terms, and quietly blind to the lives the numbers stand for.

A third panel reduces the city to coloured rectangles sliding on a grid: the same flattening, made visible. The whole system — YOLOv8s detection, a vision–language model, and a generative simulation — runs client-side on WebGPU, with no server and no cloud processing standing between the footage and the room.
