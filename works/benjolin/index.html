Benjolin

Eight-channel installation, built around a self-designed chaotic synth circuit · 2014–2015

Benjolin is the name Hartono gave to a chaotic noise-generating circuit he designed and built himself, and has used as a compositional source since at least 2011 — audible in Anti Music, and later as the chaotic voltage source driving the Buchla synthesizer in Spectral Chaos (2022). As a standalone eight-channel installation, the instrument was presented at Soundislands, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, on 10 August 2015.

A related presentation, credited to Patrick Gunawan Hartono, is also listed at Concert Two of the sines|squares Festival, John Thaw Studio Theatre, Martin Harris Centre for Music and Drama, University of Manchester (NOVARS), 24–26 October 2014 — one year earlier than the Singapore date. The instrument itself was not released as a standalone recording; it recurs as a compositional tool across other works in this practice.
