Silent Earthquake

Generative audiovisual work, with Random Brothers · 2019

A generative work melding sound, visual and video art, made with the duo Random Brothers: they composed and performed the music on modular synthesizers, while Hartono wrote the generative code driving the visuals in real time from that music.

In Random Brothers' own credits: "the music, composed by Random Brothers and played using modular, generates codes written by visual artist Patrick Hartono."
