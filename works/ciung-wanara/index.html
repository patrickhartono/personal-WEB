Live Works with Machines

Ciung Wanara

Audiovisual performance · 2023–2024

Motion captured from wayang kulit puppeteering — the Sabetan technique — is analysed through a depth camera and a machine-listening system built with FluCoMa, generating a polymorphic synthesis score directly from a centuries-old puppeteer's gesture.

The interface disappears into a much older one: the performer's instrument is not a controller designed for the piece but a technique that already existed, repurposed as a sensing surface for a real-time computer music system.

Year
2023–2024
Medium
Audiovisual performance — depth camera, FluCoMa machine listening, polymorphic synthesis
Exhibition
