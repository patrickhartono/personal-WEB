GUILTY

Performance for choir, electronics and real-time light · with Monica Lim, Morgan May, Serenata Choir ISI Yogyakarta · 2026

A cross-country collaboration for thirty voices, electronics, and a light field treated as a musical parameter rather than a stage effect. The lighting rig was scored the way the sound was: a real-time simulation of the venue built in order to compose movement, intensity and collision across the space before a single fixture was hung.

GUILTY opened ARTJOG 2026 — the highest-profile slot of the festival, and the only cross-country collaboration on the programme that night.
