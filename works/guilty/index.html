Live Works with Machines

GUILTY

Performance for choir and electronics · with Monica Lim, Morgan May, Serenata Choir ISI Yogyakarta · 2026

A cross-country collaboration built around choir, electronics and thirty performers of the Serenata Choir of ISI Yogyakarta, commissioned to open ARTJOG 2026 — the single highest-profile slot of the festival's 2026 edition, "Ars Longa: Generatio."

The electronics do not accompany the choir so much as extend it: a live system reacting to thirty voices in real time, on the one night of the festival every attending press outlet is guaranteed to cover.

Year
2026
Medium

With
