Environments You Enter

Trapped

Gamified audiovisual installation · environment design by Bayu Dirgantoro · 2019

A game environment built to be navigated by ear: players locate objects and progress through spatial listening rather than sight, with a three-dimensional sound engine standing in for the visual cues a game would ordinarily supply.

Trapped treats spatial audio as the primary interface, not an accompaniment to one — the earliest work in the record to make that argument, later extended in Hutan Plastik and Spectral Chaos.
