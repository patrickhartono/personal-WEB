Onycho Lab

Wearable fingertip prostheses · with Kaiyi Zhang and Rebecca Hall · 2026

Onycho Lab reframes the artificial fingernail as bodily technology. Five wearable fingertip prostheses — cast, sensor-laced, cabled to a forearm-mounted circuit board — extend the hand rather than decorate it, proposing a feminist hapticism in which adornment and instrumentation are the same gesture.

The work was staged as a three-day exhibition with a live participant procedure: 58 individual fittings, each documented like a minor surgical consent process — instruction card, sterile tray, consent form — a deliberate borrowing of clinical ritual for an object that is, in the end, jewellery with a nervous system.
