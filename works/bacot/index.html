Bacöt

Audiovisual work, with Random Brothers · 2022

Made with the duo Random Brothers for Extended Asia Vol. 3, "Discovery" (2022) — one entry within Islandwide Coverage, a multi-site, multi-author project by AWKNDAFFR (Wayne Lim and Soh Kay Min), shown as part of "Natasha" at Singapore Biennale 2022, Singapore Art Museum, Tanjong Pagar Distripark, 16 October 2022 – 19 March 2023.

In the artists' own words, the piece "was born in resentment towards Man's false promises and empty commitments" — made through anger and desperation "to symbolize our collective anxiety about being trapped in such an unfair and unjust society."
