Covid-19 Attack

Audiovisual collaboration with Octavianus · 2020

Made with Jakarta-based artist Octavianus for Extended Asia Vol. 2, "Satellite" — an audiovisual piece built from ambient sound and visuals "framing their emotional feelings in this pandemic situation."

Produced with support from the HEX Foundation. One of three distinct works from this period that share the pandemic as subject matter but not material — alongside the "Covid-19 (binaural)" track on Dystopia Trilogy and a separate "Covid-19 Attack" performance at BEAST FEaST, University of Birmingham (2021).
