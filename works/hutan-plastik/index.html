Hutan Plastik

Multichannel ambisonic composition · acousmatic diffusion · 2025

Hutan Plastik — "plastic forest" — is built from field recordings and processed sound, diffused multichannel across a listening room until the room itself becomes the instrument. There is no stage and no score to follow; the piece places a listener inside an environment under quiet, accumulating pressure.

The material is land and extraction in eastern Indonesia, rendered as sound rather than argument — the work does not narrate its subject so much as let the listener stand inside its consequence.
