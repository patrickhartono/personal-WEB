Post-Digital Room

Generative 3D visualization · BINUS exhibition

A generative 3D environment built around a wireframe, point-cloud human figure moving through a stylised architectural corridor, with clusters of green particle-based foliage breaking up the otherwise stark geometry — a body rendered as data, set inside a space that reads as both built and simulated.

Shown at an exhibition at Bina Nusantara University (BINUS), Jakarta.
