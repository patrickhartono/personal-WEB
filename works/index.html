About Works Research

Works

Ten featured works, 2019–2026, built on a practice reaching back to 2011 — interactive and immersive systems in which sound organises space, and machine perception is made something an audience can enter. Solo-authored unless credited otherwise. The earlier and collaborative work these grew out of is below.

Practice over time

Four practice areas, opened in sequence on top of an earlier electroacoustic and collaborative practice running since 2011 — 2019 room-scale installation · 2021 fixed-media audiovisual · 2023 live performance · 2026 machine perception. Each new mode was added on top of a running one: all four have produced work within the last 24 months.
