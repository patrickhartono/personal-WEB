Interfaces & Playable Objects

sent(i)ence

Performance-game · with Marcus Round · 2026

Two participants draw from 52 bespoke cards in a tarot reading, but the reading is not fortune-telling — each draw steers a language model's features directly, via the Neuronpedia API, so the "voice" answering the participants is a specific, engineered configuration of the model's internal representation rather than a persona written in a prompt.

The ambiguity the title names is deliberate: who is speaking when a feature-steered model answers a question shaped by a card neither participant chose freely? The game format makes that ambiguity playable rather than merely theoretical.
