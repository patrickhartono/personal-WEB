Peripersonal Modular Interfaces

Modular sensing system · 2026

A sensing system organised around peripersonal space — the zone immediately surrounding the body, rather than the body itself. Interface work developed as a means rather than an end: modules built so people can act expressively within that zone without specialised hardware or prior training.

The modularity is the argument. Rather than one fixed instrument, the system is a set of reconfigurable sensing units — the same underlying approach that recurs across the practice, from Handmate's hand-tracking to the wearable sensing in Onycho Lab.
