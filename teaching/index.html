Areas

Creative coding — Python, JavaScript, p5.js, GLSL. Real-time systems and TouchDesigner. Interactive and generative visuals. Creative machine learning. Physical computing and sensor-based interaction. Spatial audio and sound design for interaction. Audiovisual composition and performance. XR and immersive media. Practice-based research methods.

Pedagogical development — TD-EDUPack

A teaching resource addressing a specific, recurring difficulty: students stall when they first meet node-based programming environments. TD-EDUPack was presented as a panellist contribution at the ACM SIGGRAPH Education Committee’s SOIREE 2026 symposium. I have taught TouchDesigner in studio and workshop settings since 2017.
